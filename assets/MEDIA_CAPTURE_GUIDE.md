## Part 1: The 4 High-Impact Screenshots to Capture

Non-technical recruiters decide within 10 seconds whether a project is real. Capturing these four clean screenshots and saving them into the `assets/` folder will immediately populate the visual previews in `README.md`.

### Screenshot 1: `assets/scraper-dashboard-preview.png`
- **Which App to Open:** Local Job Scraper / AI Discovery Dashboard (or the clean UI view).
- **What to Show on Screen:**
  - A filtered table or card grid showing 4 to 6 local job postings.
  - Clearly visible **AI Match Score badges** (e.g., `94/100 Match`, `88/100 Match`), extracted hourly pay (`$21.50–$24.00/hr`), and green/amber **Barrier Flags** (`On Bus Route`, `Paid Training`, `No Degree Required`).
- **Why Recruiters Care:** Proves you don't just run command-line scripts—you deliver clean, scannable decision tools for operations staff.

### Screenshot 2: `assets/sheets-export-preview.png`
- **Which App to Open:** The synced Google Sheets / Staff Digest output generated by the Job Scraper pipeline.
- **What to Show on Screen:**
  - Standardized columns: `Date Added`, `Job Title`, `Employer`, `Verified Wage`, `Match Score`, `Barrier Notes`, and `Staff Approval Status`.
  - Conditional formatting on the Match Score column (green for `80+`, yellow for `60–79`).
- **Why Recruiters Care:** Customer Success and Operations hiring managers live in spreadsheets. Showing an automated pipeline that lands cleanly in Google Sheets proves zero friction for frontline teams.

### Screenshot 3: `assets/cms-portal-preview.png`
- **Which App to Open:** WRTP-CMS (Case Management Portal running in Demo / Seed Data mode).
- **What to Show on Screen:**
  - The main Case Manager or Partner Coordinator dashboard showing **synthetic participant tokens** (never real names), milestone progress bars (`Intake Verified -> Resume Built -> Placed`), and active partner network counts (`35+ Active Partners / 85+ Network`).
- **Why Recruiters Care:** Demonstrates enterprise-grade workflow thinking, role-based privacy, and multi-partner coordination.

### Screenshot 4: `assets/career-hub-preview.png`
- **Which App to Open:** GroundWork / SolidStep Guided Resume & Career Hub.
- **What to Show on Screen:**
  - The step-by-step guided resume builder side-by-side with the barrier-filtered job matching view (`Transit Accessible`, `Day Shift`, `Training Provided`).
- **Why Recruiters Care:** Shows empathy for end-user onboarding—a core metric in Customer Success, EdTech, and HealthTech roles.

## Part 2: Word-for-Word 90-Second Loom Video Script

Record this using Loom (Camera bubble in bottom-left corner + Screen Share). Keep your energy calm, direct, and conversational.

### [0:00 – 0:15] The Hook: Who I Am & Why I Build (Start on `docs/index.html`)
> "Hi, I'm Brian Cummings. For the last 15-plus years across e-commerce operations, adult education, and clinical rehabilitation, my job has come down to one thing: finding the manual bottlenecks that slow teams down and building practical systems to fix them.
>
> In the next 75 seconds, I'll show you two tools I built to eliminate repetitive staff busywork."

### [0:15 – 0:45] Tool 1: AI Job Discovery & Matching Pipeline (Switch to Tab 2: Job Scraper UI / Sheet)
> "First, our job developers were spending over eight hours every week manually hunting through regional job boards and reading long descriptions just to check pay rates and physical requirements.
>
> I built an automated Python and Gemini AI pipeline that collects regional listings overnight and grades every post against a strict checklist. Instead of guessing, the AI is locked into a structured schema—extracting the exact hourly wage, checking bus route access, and flagging barriers like heavy lifting or license requirements.
>
> By 8:00 AM, staff get this scored dashboard. That cut manual search labor by over 80 percent and gave our team six-plus hours back every week to coach clients."

### [0:45 – 1:15] Tool 2: HIPAA-Aligned Case Management Portal (Switch to Tab 3: WRTP-CMS Demo View)
> "Second, we needed to coordinate client intake and state grant reporting across a network of more than 85 partner organizations—without passing spreadsheets back and forth over email.
>
> I built this role-based web portal to bridge our legacy FileMaker records with a modern cloud workspace. Staff enter a milestone once, private case notes stay separated from state grant metrics, and managers can audit 30-day onboarding progress in real time with zero double data entry."

### [1:15 – 1:30] The Close: How This Applies to Your Team (Switch back to `docs/index.html`)
> "Whether I'm cutting client onboarding time by 50 percent in e-commerce or automating compliance workflows across 85 partners, I build tools that help teams move faster and help customers succeed in their first 30 days.
>
> Feel free to explore the interactive page below, or reach out directly—I'd love to talk about what your operations team is building next."
